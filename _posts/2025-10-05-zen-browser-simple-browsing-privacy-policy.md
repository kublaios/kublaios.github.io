Zen Browser | Simple browsing (the "Application") operated by Kubilay Erdogan (the "Service Provider") is committed to safeguarding your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the Application.

## Information Collection and Use

The Application may collect the following information:

- Device IP address
- Pages viewed, including visit time and duration
- Total time spent in the Application
- Mobile operating system

The Application does not gather precise device location.

## How Your Data Is Used

- To provide and improve services, send important notices, and surface relevant marketing promotions
- To share only aggregated, anonymized data with trusted third parties for analytics and service optimization
- To integrate with third-party services, each governed by its own policies, including [AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en) and [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

## Opt-Out and Retention

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the Application. To request deletion of stored data, email [upgrade-armbinde7a@icloud.com](mailto:upgrade-armbinde7a@icloud.com).

## Children

The Application does not target children under 13, nor does it knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact the Service Provider to have it removed.

## Security

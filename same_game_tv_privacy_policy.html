Same Game TV Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Same Game TV is an ad-free, paid Apple TV game developed by Kubilay Erdogan. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data Collection

Same Game TV does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your game preferences and high score locally on your Apple TV. This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you sign in to Game Center and submit a new high score, the app sends that score to Apple's Game Center service for the Same Game leaderboard. Apple may associate and display the score with your Game Center profile according to your Game Center settings. The developer does not use Game Center information for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Apple's handling of Game Center information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Same Game TV does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
